## Practical Applications

Conditional statements allow programs to respond differently depending on the information they receive.

They are essential for interactive applications, validation systems, financial calculations, and automation.

In cybersecurity, conditional logic is frequently used to evaluate security events, identify suspicious activity, and determine whether certain conditions require additional investigation.
