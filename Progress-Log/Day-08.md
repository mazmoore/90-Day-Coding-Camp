## Practical Applications

While loops allow programs to repeat operations until a specific condition is met.

They are useful for interactive applications, inventory management, menu systems, input handling, and automated processing.

In cybersecurity, loops can support log processing, monitoring tasks, alert handling, and repetitive administrative operations.
